Our Story

Dedicated to the preservation and innovation of sacred choral music.

Japhet Onipaba Mireku

Japhet Onipaba Mireku is a renowned composer and conductor whose work bridges the gap between traditional sacred music and contemporary African choral expression. With over 20 years of experience leading choirs across the continent, his compositions are celebrated for their spiritual depth and intricate harmonies.

His musical journey began in the local church choir, where he discovered his passion for harmonizing voices. Today, Onipaba Music serves as a platform to share this gift with the world, offering a vast library of original compositions and arrangements that inspire worship and reflection.
